*NB: Google Reader is long gone, so these links to the Observations page have been removed. I’m sorry too.*

Following in the [footsteps of giants](http://smarterware.org/remainders), I’ve decided to create a separate page to track my notes on shared items from Google Reader. One reason for this is to encourage me to comment on GReader items rather than save them until I have time to write full-fledged blog posts. The only issue I see with the current system is that there’s no easy way to direct items more appropriate for [Simplifying Complexity](http://www.thoughtstorm.com/)/ThoughtStorm or [ASDworld](http://www.asdworld.com/) to those sites.

The new page, Observations, is available from the header throughout [rickcolosimo.com](/). Depending on the amount of feedback from those items, I may try to integrate them into the regular posts for anyone viewing the RSS feed.

Let me know what you think.
