Here’s a [pair of instructive quotes](https://medium.com/@manish_demblani/bad-code-a-deadly-nightmare-8994233655b0), ostensibly about code:

> Clean code always looks like it was written by someone who cares.”

– Robert C. Martin, Clean Code: A Handbook of Agile Software Craftsmanship

This is what I see when I look at contracts.  
And processes.  
And business models.  
Caring doesn’t scale. #StartupRules

> It was the bad code that brought the company down.

– Robert C. Martin, Clean Code: A Handbook of Agile Software Craftsmanship

That seldom happens with contracts (they’re usually a symptom, not a disease), but it definitely happens with bad processes or weak business models.  
It never happens with caring.
